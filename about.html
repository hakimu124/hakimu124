Our Story

A Taste of East Africa

Bringing the rich flavors and traditions of Kenya together in one unforgettable dining experience

Founded in 2016

Our Journey

Bava Restaurant was born from a simple dream: to share the incredible flavors of East Africa with everyone who walks through our doors. What started as a small family kitchen in Thika has grown into three beloved locations serving hundreds of customers daily.

Our name "Bava" means "father" in several East African languages — a tribute to the patriarch of our founding family, whose recipes and passion for cooking inspired it all.

Food is more than sustenance — it's a way to bring people together, to share culture, and to create lasting memories.

— Our Founder
